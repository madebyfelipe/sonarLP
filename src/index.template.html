    Questions

    Ask Sonar directly
    How is Sonar different from a standard website chatbot?

    A standard chatbot answers questions from a script. Sonar asks them. It follows a diagnostic framework, works out where the prospect's funnel is leaking, and only then recommends a next step and books the call.

    Can I match the widget to my brand?

    Yes. Colors, typography, avatar, tone of voice and widget position all follow your brand kit. Most teams match their site in one sitting.

    Which CRMs and calendar tools does it work with?

    HubSpot, Pipedrive, Salesforce and Twenty for CRM. Cal.com and Calendly for booking. Slack and WhatsApp for alerts and follow-up. Anything else connects through n8n or webhooks.

    What happens with visitors who aren't a fit?

    Sonar stays helpful and points them to the right resource: a guide, a case study or a lower tier. They don't land on your reps' calendars, and the conversation still goes into your CRM.

    Will it ever promise ROI or invent prices?

    No. Sonar only quotes prices from the pricing sheet you give it and never promises results. When it doesn't know something, it says so and offers a human.

    How fast can we put it on our landing pages?

    It's one script tag. Paste it into Webflow, Framer, WordPress or any HTML page and Sonar is live. Training it on your playbook usually takes an afternoon.
